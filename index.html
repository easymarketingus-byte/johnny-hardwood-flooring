Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about our hardwood floor services.

Do you do ceilings or general remodeling?
No, we do not do ceilings or drywall. We specialize strictly and 100% in hardwood floors: new hardwood floor installation, sanding & refinishing existing floors, and repairing damaged wood boards. This focus ensures you receive true flooring expertise.
Are your estimates really free with no obligation?
Yes, 100% free. Johnny comes to inspect your floors in person, takes measurements, answers your questions, and gives you a straightforward written quote. No high pressure, ever.
Are you fully insured?
Yes, Johnny Hardwood Flooring is fully insured with comprehensive liability insurance for both residential and commercial projects.
Can damaged or stained floor boards be repaired without replacing the whole floor?
Yes! We can selectively replace broken, buckled, or water-damaged planks by weaving in matching wood boards, then sanding and staining to blend seamlessly with the rest of your room.

You can call Johnny directly at (207) 812-1455 or our alternate number (207) 667-7070. If we are running sanding equipment, please leave a voicemail and Johnny will call you right back!
