Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before restoring or installing your hardwood floors.

How long does hardwood floor refinishing typically take?
Most typical residential projects (300 to 800 sq ft) take between 2 to 4 days, depending on sanding depth, repairs needed, stain color drying time, and number of protective top coats. We will give you an exact schedule during your free estimate.
Are your estimates really free with no obligation?
Yes, 100% free! Johnny will inspect your floors, measure the area, evaluate existing wear, answer all your questions, and give you an honest price in writing. No pressure and no hidden fees.
Are you fully insured for both residential and commercial work?
Yes, Johnny Hardwood Flooring is fully insured. We carry liability coverage protecting your residential home or commercial property throughout the duration of the project.
Can you fix floors that have water damage or deep dog scratches?
In most cases, yes! Surface scratches sand right out. If specific boards are rotten, stained black from prolonged moisture, or split, we can selectively weave in replacement hardwood boards before sanding and staining for a seamless look.
Which phone number should I call?
You can reach us at either (207) 812-1455 or (207) 667-7070. If we are operating sanding machines, please leave a brief voicemail or submit our online form, and Johnny will return your call promptly.
